# Code of Conduct

Status: **draft**.

The Eiraman project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, by reference:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

The Covenant applies in all project spaces (repositories, issues, discussions, meetings, chat channels, events) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement contacts

Report incidents privately to:

- Code of Conduct team: `<conduct@PLACEHOLDER>`
- Alternative contact, if a member of the team is involved: `<conduct-alternate@PLACEHOLDER>`

Reports are handled confidentially. Members of the team step out of any case in which they are involved or have a conflict of interest (GOVERNANCE §8).

## Enforcement responsibility

- **Stage 1:** the Steward with at least one other maintainer, so that no decision is taken by one person alone.
- **Stage 2 and later:** a Code of Conduct team of three appointed by the Steering Committee, from at least two organisations.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant Enforcement Guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban). Decisions are recorded with written reasons and may be appealed once to the Steering Committee (Steward in Stage 1), excluding members involved.
